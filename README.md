# Walter Endrizzi

**Machine Learning Engineer & Data Scientist | PhD Candidate**

I am a Data Scientist and Machine Learning professional currently completing my PhD at Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK) and the University of Trento. My research and engineering focus centers on developing machine learning pipelines, trustworthy AI frameworks, and deep learning models for complex, real-world clinical datasets.

I specialize in writing robust, production-ready code. I am experienced in utilizing HPC clusters, Slurm, and Docker to ensure scalable and reproducible experiments, bridging the gap between algorithmic research and clinical applications.

### Featured Work & Research
* **Uncertainty Quantification in Clinical Settings:** Engineered frameworks to address standard uncertainty quantification failures under extreme clinical class imbalance, including patient risk-ranking algorithms based on rescaled decision entropy ($h_\tau$). *(Accepted at [BioData Mining](https://github.com/endrwalter/uncertainty_ml), [code](https://github.com/endrwalter/uncertainty_ml))*
* **Generative AI for Healthcare:** Provided core software engineering and repository architecture support for a diffusion model enabling cross-modal synthesis of missing clinical data modalities. *(Published in [PLOS Computational Biology](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013455), [code](https://github.com/r-marchesi/coherent-genAI))*
* **Predictive Modeling for Neurodegenerative Diseases:** Developed robust clinical machine learning pipelines for Parkinson's Disease and Multiple Sclerosis prognosis using real-world clinical data. *(Published in [npj Parkinson's Disease](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-026-01550-1), [European Journal of Neurology](https://doi.org/10.1111/ene.70417), [code](https://github.com/endrwalter/pd_motor_symptoms))*
*  **PRISM (Platform for Real-time Interoperable Smart Monitoring):** Co-developed a smart clinical dashboard, collaborating with neurologists to integrate AI risk predictions into real-world workflows. *([Read more here](https://www.cooperazionetrentina.it/it/news/poc-by-trentino))*
